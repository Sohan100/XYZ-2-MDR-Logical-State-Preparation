**Where the cost comes from.**
- **Threshold.** With noiseless links and no idling, XYZ^2 ties XZZX at a threshold ratio of 1.013(30) (docs/data/campaign/gap/fractions.csv; C22).
  - Idling accounts for 0.60(1) of the gap.
  - Link noise accounts for 0.23(2) against XZZX without idling, or 0.43(4) against XZZX under SD6.
  - The two effects are super-additive.
- **Idling.** The idling comes from the deeper schedule that one-ancilla weight-6 checks need.
  - XYZ^2 has 8 ticks per round against 6.
  - At 99/97 qubits it has 254 gate-layer idle sites per round against 52: 130 on data qubits, 100 on link ancillas waiting through 4 of the 6 gate layers, and 24 on plaquette ancillas (C25).
- **Measurement noise.** Noise on the weight-6 measurements does not limit the threshold (C34).
- **Below threshold.** The 2x qubit overhead (4d^2-1 against 2d^2-1 qubits, so 1/sqrt2 of the distance at equal qubits) is an independent cause of the loss (C4, C25).

**Conclusion (inference).** XYZ^2 is not a useful memory or r = 1 preparation code for any modelled hardware.
- Its leads are confined to two idealized models with BM decoding. The device-like variants remove those leads (f = 0.02) or turn them into a nominal loss (run E).
- One fixed-footprint window remains open, because its deciding competitors were not run.
- The paper's contribution is the fault-tolerant MDR protocol and its decoders.
