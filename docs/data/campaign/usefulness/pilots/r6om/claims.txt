Every XYZ^2 lead that is significant with Poisson errors also gets its scaled value. Ties stay ties when scaled, and ratios above 1 use Poisson errors.

With BM decoding, XYZ^2 has a lower error per round than square XZZX in its distance-d frame. At 99/97 qubits the lead is significant under both conventions at p_T 6% and at r = 6d. - Of the decoders run under hw_tr, only BM gives the lead. With corr_gauge and MWPM it is a loss at the one point probed (below). The repository's other decoders (cfe, bp_corr, tesseract, the TN decoders) were not run under hw_tr, so whether they widen or remove the edge is untested.

- Surface-code QEC-optimal readout (5.8 us): p_R 2.7%, p_RR 3.1%, p_T 1.5%.

Two tested mixtures are proxies of these settings. Both omit reset error, so their readout flip matches the device's p_R, not its p_RR. Both have no gate-layer dephasing (f = 0).
- m1 (p_T 4%, p_R 1%, pG 0.1%) is the maximum-fidelity proxy, with p_T about 26% too high. There XYZ^2 BM is 0.70(7) of XZZX: 1.34e-3 against 1.92e-3 per round. That is 4.1 sigma (stat only; Poisson errors scaled by 1.66 for scatter across four replicates).
- E (p_T 1.5%, p_R 2.7%, pG 0.1%) is the QEC-optimal proxy. There XYZ^2 is 1.72(16)x worse: 1.13e-3 against 6.60e-4 per round. That is 4.4 sigma (stat only, Poisson). Its two replicates agree (chi2 0.01 on 1 dof), so no scale is applied. With the scatter scale pooled over the three replicated conditions (1.52), it is 1.72(25), 2.9 sigma, just short of the 3-sigma loss criterion.

Comparing each code at its best tested point, XZZX (6.60e-4, at E) is 1.7x below XYZ^2 (1.13e-3, at E). To match it, XYZ^2 would need an error at least 1.7x below both of its tested values (inference). Untested:
- XYZ^2 at the exact maximum-fidelity point (p_T 3.2%, p_RR 1.6%). It has less idling and more readout error than m1, so the direction of the change is unknown.
- XZZX at its own Eq. 14 readout time: 7.5 us with Hetenyi-Wootton's Table I plane, about 7.9 us with this repository's corners (hw_tr idling placement).
- XYZ^2 at its own Eq. 14 time with this repository's corners, about 7.3 us. With Table I it is 5.7 us, close to E.

Gate-layer dephasing removes the m1 lead. With dephasing at f = 0.02 of p_T on every gate-layer idle site, the m1 ratio is 1.02(13) (stat only, scaled for replicate scatter), a tie. f between 0 and 0.02 is untested. Hetenyi-Wootton give no gate durations. For an assumed 100 ns gate layer without echo, f is about 0.008-0.03 at the maximum-fidelity readout and 0.011-0.07 at the QEC-optimal readout (inference).

These runs also omit reset error, single-qubit gate error and the readout-pair two-qubit error of Hetenyi-Wootton's model. Each acts on both codes, and their relative effect is untested. The runs also omit the second readout round per cycle in Hetenyi-Wootton's XYZ^2 layout, which adds idling to XYZ^2 only (inference; C37).
