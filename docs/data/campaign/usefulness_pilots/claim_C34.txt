- Its equal-data-qubit advantage is code capacity at eta = 10 with near-ML (EWD) decoding, against XZZX in its distance-d frame. Reading its Fig. 7a inset: about 1.7x at p = 0.22 to 2.7x at 0.16, at 121 data qubits.
